Pixonal today announced Fusion, the next iteration of its governed-intelligence platform, in tandem with a strategic joint venture with Dubai Land Department (DLD).

## A New Operating Layer for Public-Sector Intelligence

Fusion combines Pixonal's semantic governance layer with a real-time decision fabric — giving public-sector teams a single, verifiable source of truth across operations, policy, and citizen-facing services.

## The DLD Partnership

The joint venture formalises Pixonal's collaboration with DLD around real-estate registration, sector intelligence, and innovation programmes, building on prior work recognised at DLD's 2024 Stakeholders Forum.
