Pixonal today released Llumen 2.0, the next major iteration of its governed-intelligence platform. The release expands enterprise connectivity, strengthens governance controls, and ships a refreshed analyst workspace.

## What's New

- Native connectors for major data warehouses and lakehouses
- Fine-grained, attribute-based access controls
- A reworked analyst workspace with collaborative semantics

## Why It Matters

Llumen 2.0 makes it materially easier for organisations to operate one shared, verifiable picture of their data across teams — without sacrificing governance.

For more information about Pixonal, visit [www.pixonal.com](https://www.pixonal.com).
