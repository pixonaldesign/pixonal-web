Dubai Land Department (DLD) recognised more than 120 entities for outstanding contribution to the real estate sector across the Emirate. The honourees span developers, brokerages, technology providers, and innovation partners shaping how property, transactions, and citizen services operate in Dubai.

## A Sector-Wide Recognition

DLD's recognition programme highlights organisations contributing to transparency, service quality, and innovation across the real-estate value chain — from registration and licensing to investor experience and data-driven planning.

## Pixonal Among the Honourees

Pixonal was named for its role supporting governed intelligence and decision systems across the sector, helping teams move from fragmented data to a shared, verifiable picture of operations and outcomes.
